01 · THE IDEA

What if autocomplete were visual?

Keys that can't continue your word disappear instantly, and the remaining keys expand to fill the space. You see your options shrink in real time: bigger keys, fewer typos. MergeKey learns the words you teach it, and Merge Mode can be switched off if you only want the disappearing keys.

  1. 01

    Gestures that teach

    Swipe up to teach a new word, left to delete a whole word, right to paste. A key heatmap shows where your thumb actually lands.

  2. 02

    Made for iPad

    Full-size layouts, pinch to split the keyboard for thumb typing, and a two-finger trackpad to move the cursor.

  3. 03

    Make it yours

    14 styles from Liquid Glass to Typewriter and Neon, custom colours, 6 key fonts and three keyboard heights.

03 · PRIVACY

Private by design

MergeKey never connects to the internet and doesn't collect or transmit what you type; everything stays on your device. Each of the 31 languages has native prediction and accented variants, built on the Leipzig Corpora Collection.

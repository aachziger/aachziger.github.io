01 · THE IDEA

How fast can one thumb really go?

Tap the correct side of the screen to send each ball into the bin of the same colour. Miss once and it's over. The longer you survive, the faster the balls drop, until even your thumb can't keep up.

  1. 01

    Built for one hand

    Every input is a tap on the left or right half of the screen. Instant start: no menus, no tutorial, no gestures to learn.

  2. 02

    Versus like table tennis

    Best of 3, 5 or 11 against a friend over Bluetooth, or anyone in the world via Game Center. Winner serves, loser waits.

  3. 03

    Leaderboards & achievements

    Chase your high score on the Game Center leaderboard and unlock achievements as you level up.
